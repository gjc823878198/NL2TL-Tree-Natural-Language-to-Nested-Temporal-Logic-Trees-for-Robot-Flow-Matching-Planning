NL2TL-Tree: Natural Language to Nested Temporal-Logic Trees for Robustness-Guided Flow-Matching Planning

Demo video — a phone-typed command becomes a checked spatiotemporal task for a mobile robot.

UbiComp / ISWC 2026 · Posters & Demos · Shanghai, October 11–15

What you are watching. A natural-language task is parsed by a frozen LLM into a nested Signal Temporal Logic (STL) tree, rendered to a differentiable robustness ρ, and planned by a frozen flow-matching planner (TeLoGraF) under robustness-gradient guidance. The trajectory is executed closed-loop on a simulated TurtleBot3 (Gazebo) with an online sound robustness monitor that stops and re-plans on ρ ≤ 0. Early-stage prototype; the reported evaluation is a small parsing pilot and a six-task simulation feasibility study.
